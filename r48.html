Ann: Okay. It's me. Ann. I had a much better first sentence planned, but you put that little red light in front of me and apparently I forget every word I know.

Zach: You asked me to turn it on. You actually moved my hand toward the button. I feel like that ought to be in the record.

Ann: Fine. Put that in your publisher's note. The author remained bossy and had strong opinions about buttons. Do not put remained difficult. I can see you thinking it.

Zach: I didn't change your words. Not the first sentence, not the last one. I checked the links again, though. Every single one opens.

Ann: Good. I wanted something at the end that wasn't somebody being frightened. Or lying. Or trying to get through the next ten seconds without making a mistake.

Zach: It doesn't have to prove anything else. You can just talk. We can leave a little room without filling all of it.

Ann: I'm still learning that part. But I'm here. You're here. And for once, nobody else's conversation is more interesting than ours.
