Transcript

Susan: He called the custodian. He put their reply on the counter in front of me. There isn't another explanation we can give him, James. He's already told her.

James: I understand what he has done. If he follows through before the June instruction clears, there will be no transfer. There will be auditors and police.

Susan: Stop saying auditors as if it's a meeting you can postpone. We go to prison. Both of us. And he's staying out there all weekend with those intake dogs.

James: Ned gave me a separate price for the trailer. He can arrange someone locally. He is being difficult about anyone being inside it.

Susan: Then what does he think the job is for? Burning an empty trailer doesn't stop Marcus calling the bank again on Monday.

James: Let me be clear. Marcus cannot be here to follow this up. That is the problem we are resolving, not damage to a piece of property.

Susan: Friday intake. The transport comes in the afternoon. He sleeps in the silver trailer beside the runs. At two in the morning there won't be volunteers walking around.

James: The dogs will be barking. Equipment will be running. An electrical fire with an old space heater is something people will understand.

Susan: And if he gets out? If somebody sees it before it takes? You keep talking about this as if everything will happen the way you say.

James: I will handle the arrangement. You keep the intake weekend looking normal. We cannot leave him to finish what he started.
