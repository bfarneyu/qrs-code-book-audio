Listen: Emily tells Paul

From The QRS Code Book by Bryan Farney

Transcript

Paul: Start with what you heard yourself, Mrs. Whitlock. Not what anyone explained after the arrests.

Emily: I was dusting outside the study in April. Mrs. Caldwell was speaking to Mr. Pell about the new will. The Center was to receive nine million dollars.

Paul: And you told Susan. What did you mean to tell her?

Emily: That her grandmother had made up her mind. Susan kept asking about loans and her inheritance. I wanted her to stop pestering Mrs. Caldwell. I walked over to the carriage house.

Paul: Did you know about the missing money, or that anyone intended to replace the will?

Emily: No, sir. Neither. I know what my old conviction makes people think. I did not forge anything for my son.

Paul: Now Jack's call to Ann. Who told you he made it?

Emily: Jack did. Monday, at the jail. He said the mechanic found the recorder, Susan found Ann's details on the volunteer sheet, and James made him telephone her. He was crying behind the glass. That is what he told me.
