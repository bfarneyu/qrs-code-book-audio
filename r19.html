Listen: Paul separates the two jobs

From The QRS Code Book by Bryan Farney

Transcript

Paul: Two jobs, Zach. Go back to the mill. Ned names the will. Then he starts on the instruction, and James stops him. Those are separate papers.

Zach: We thought instruction meant instructions for putting the will in the safe.

Paul: The later calls distinguish them. The will bears May tenth. Placement is Thursday, May sixteenth. A date on a page isn't the date someone enters a house.

Zach: And June belongs to the transfer. Six hundred thousand. Her last liquid money, not the nine-million-dollar gift.

Paul: A forged authorization to the custodian. Money into James's control, enough for a partial payment to the Dades while the Center waits.

Zach: So Friday wasn't the deadline we thought it was.

Paul: Correct. And misunderstanding a deadline doesn't excuse tapping his telephone. Preserve the files. Don't mistake knowing something for having usable evidence.
