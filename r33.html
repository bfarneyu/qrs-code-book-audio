Transcript

Ann: You did it again on that call. You told him what he was asking for, and you wouldn't let him answer any other question first.

Ned: People change their minds about what they said. Especially people with lawyers. They remember you offering things they asked you for three times.

Ann: At the first meeting, you said two papers, their people handling placement, you staying out of the house. Then you made him agree out loud.

Ned: I restate the job I'm being paid for. Then I ask, is that what you want? Say yes plainly. Not a nod. Not whatever you think. A plain yes.

Ann: Because you're recording. That's why you're letting this run now. You want a record of what you told me, not just what you told him.

Ned: Two stretches taught me something. A rich man hires your hands, then says your hands acted on their own. I keep the words in an encrypted folder.

Ann: Then keep mine too. I didn't agree to come here. I didn't agree to stay. Whatever you decide next, that doesn't change.

Ned: I know what I did. You don't need to explain it to the phone. What you need to understand is what he's asking me to do now.

Ann: Show me. If you're deciding what I'm worth, I need to hear the price.
