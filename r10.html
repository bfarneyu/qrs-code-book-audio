Ann: That's the carriage house on the right. Under the carport. The silver crossover. Same one from the restaurant.

Zach: I see it. Don't lean all the way across me. People can see through windows.

Ann: I'm looking at a house. People look at houses. Can you see the keypad?

Zach: Left pillar, on the metal arm. No white pickup. No Lexus either, at least from here.

Ann: He knew the rooms and the evening schedule. If he doesn't live here, somebody still lets him in.

Zach: Or he knows the code. We don't know which. We can't see the whole property.

Ann: Okay. Write down what we can see, not what I want it to mean.

Zach: Good. Hood down. We're leaving.
