From The QRS Code Book by Bryan Farney

Transcript

Frank: Paul, I've got a student on my porch with something you ought to look at when you're back.

Paul: Something for a newspaper or something for a detective?

Frank: Possibly both. Edith Caldwell's granddaughter and her money man arranging a forged household document. An out-of-town paper man, and Jack Whitlock helping with access.

Paul: Who told you that?

Frank: The young woman sitting here. Ann Delgado. She heard them. Says she has recordings and photographs.

Paul: Do we have the original?

Frank: She says yes. I haven't audited them. That's why I'm calling you.

Paul: Thursday morning. Bring the recording devices, original cards, photographs. No edited highlights. And tell her not to collect anything else while she waits.

Frank: She can hear you. I'll say it again anyway.
