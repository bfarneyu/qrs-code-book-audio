From The QRS Code Book by Bryan Farney

Transcript

Zach: Hey, is that lower pasture where we're supposed to park? I think I came in the wrong way.

Jack: Past those cones. Keep the lane open for folks coming out.

Zach: Thanks. You work for the Center?

Jack: Just help when they need hands. Moving things. Gates. Whatever they've got.

Zach: Looks like they've kept you busy today.

Jack: This isn't much. Mostly errands for my stepson. Take his Lexus to the dealer for service. Save him the trip.

Zach: Nice car to run errands in. I'll get out of your lane.

Jack: Watch that ditch when you turn. Deeper than it looks.
