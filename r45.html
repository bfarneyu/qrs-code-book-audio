Zach: Ann never let go of a question once she got her teeth into it. Sometimes I would be explaining something and realize she was already working out the next thing to ask.

Zach: I wish I had better words for what she means to me. I keep trying to make this sound like Ann. She'd tell me to stop explaining and let somebody else talk.

Priya: If I'd gone out five minutes earlier, maybe things would have been different. Then she came home, and I thought we had her back. I thought we were allowed to stop being scared.
