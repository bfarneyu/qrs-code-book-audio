Transcript

Ann: When Marcus told you what the bank said, you weren't surprised. Had somebody warned you about James before that?

Edith: The bank logo warned me. A year ago, on one of those simplified statements Susan brought me. The bank had changed it after the merger. James apparently had not.

Ann: You knew for a year? Why didn't you ask the bank yourself, or tell Susan you thought something was wrong?

Edith: I suspected. That is different. I did not know whether Susan was being deceived. Accuse a man too soon and he destroys the paper, then apologizes for a computer error.

Ann: So the Center gift made him produce money he couldn't just put on a statement.

Edith: After my March visit, I saw Pell in April. Nine million in the will, with a million and a half needed for construction before summer. Real money. Marcus had eyes as well as qualifications.

Ann: And the signed copy behind the hunting prints? You wanted to see who panicked?

Edith: Pell had the original. I knew where that was. The counterpart in my safe might tell me something about the people in my house.

Ann: Did you think they might hurt somebody?

Edith: I expected a lawsuit, child. Perhaps a confession of debt. Not a fire. Not those marks on your wrists. I did not foresee that.
