Transcript

Ann: Is this working? Say something so I know it isn't just recording me.

Zach: It's working. Your level is good. Don't tap the microphone.

Ann: I wasn't going to tap it. I was pointing.

Zach: Point farther away. Now talk at your normal volume.

Ann: This is my normal volume. You're the one making it complicated.

Zach: Good. Both voices are clear. That's the test.
