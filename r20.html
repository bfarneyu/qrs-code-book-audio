Transcript

James: I have ten minutes before someone comes in. If this is about Thursday, nothing has changed. There is no reason to keep discussing it.

Susan: I'm at the office. I'm not calling about Thursday. Marcus wants another breakdown, and he isn't asking the committee anymore. He's asking her directly.

James: He trains dogs. He can ask for whatever he likes. She has a financial adviser, and it is not Marcus Tolliver.

Susan: You keep saying that like it stops him. He has a folder now. Actual paper, with little tabs. Hold on. No, I'm still here.

James: What exactly is in the folder? Don't tell me what you think. Tell me what you've actually seen him carrying.

Susan: He's comparing the statements I sent, the ones you sent, with whatever the Center was promised. Don't start on me. You know which papers I mean.

James: Then stop helping him collect them. If he asks you for anything, refer him to me. Do not invent an explanation he can check.

Susan: That's easy to say when you're not there watching him lean over her chair. Call him yourself. I'm tired of being the person who keeps everybody calm.
