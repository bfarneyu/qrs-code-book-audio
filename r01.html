Transcript

James: We can't risk it anymore. Every time you tell me to wait, something else changes. I need you to understand that waiting is a decision too.

Susan: Don't give me your office voice. I'm the one who has to sit across from her and act like everything is perfectly normal.

James: Then stop saying murder where somebody can hear you. You know what she would do if she found out. Neither of us walks away from that.

Susan: And your mother can't know. Not a hint, not some little reassurance you think she'll keep to herself. She notices when you reassure her.

James: I haven't told her anything. Let me be clear. This meeting is only to settle what can be done and what it will cost us.

Susan: Next week, then. Friday the twenty-sixth, not tomorrow. You said somewhere nobody would wander past and recognize your car. Where does he expect us?

James: I'll drive. Don't put it in your calendar. Just remember: old mill, Friday, six.
