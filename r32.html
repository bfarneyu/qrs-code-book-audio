Transcript

Paul: He knows the motel is compromised. An occupied farm gives him witnesses. Where does a man from San Antonio hide a car here without asking somebody's permission?

Ruiz: An empty building with access off a secondary road. Covered doors. Enough room to drive the Malibu inside.

Paul: Start with places he already knows. The original mill meeting gives us River Road. The old agricultural properties south of town give us other covered bays.

Ruiz: They're on the list. We haven't established which one, or whether he's moved since last night.

Paul: If we send marked units up every drive, he'll see them coming across open gravel.

Ruiz: That's why we're checking approaches quietly. Nobody rushes a building because it fits a theory. We need the car, a witness, something current.

Paul: I'll keep the original locations beside the map. Tell me which files you need.
