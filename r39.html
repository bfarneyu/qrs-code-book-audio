Listen: Ann and Zach, afterward

From The QRS Code Book by Bryan Farney

Open or download the MP3 recording

Transcript

Zach: I keep looking at your wrists. I brought you all of it. The dish, the contact mike, that stupid forty-dollar box. Then I found the link.

Ann: You didn't bring me to the Olive Garden. I was already recording before you knew anything.

Zach: I could have said no over the tater tots. One word. Instead I started explaining how to hear people across seventy yards.

Ann: And I asked you to. Don't make me the passenger in this. Who wanted Room 212? Who kept asking Marcus questions?

Zach: The magnet got you identified. Jack found something I built, and then Ned was waiting in your driveway.

Ann: I chose to go back. You asked me not to. I stood at your door and explained why my terrible idea was reasonable.

Zach: I don't know how to stop thinking about what could have happened.

Ann: Neither do I. Sit down, please. You can hold my hand. Just don't squeeze the tape.
