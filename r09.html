Transcript

Carol: Did you get the oil checked, or are you waiting for the engine light to make the appointment?

Ann: It's fine, Mom. The car's fine. I'm studying. I have a lot of work.

Carol: How are your grades? You sound like you're doing three things while you're talking to me.

Ann: They're good. I'm just on the computer. Public records for school. Nothing exciting.

Carol: Don't walk to your car alone after dark. And that phone your father bought is for lectures, correct?

Ann: Yes. Schoolwork. I promise. You make it sound like I'm chasing serial killers.

Carol: You're going to get yourself killed.

Ann: I'm at my desk, Mom. I'll be careful. Get some sleep before your shift.
