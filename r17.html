Listen: The service entrance

From The QRS Code Book by Bryan Farney

Transcript

Jack: You can't stand there. Delivery truck comes around that corner, driver won't see you till he's right on top of you.

Ann: I'm sorry. Something fell off our car. It's just beside your tire. I'll pick it up and get out of your way.

Jack: Just leave it where it lays. Got glass all through that gravel now. Somebody'll come with a broom in a minute.

Ann: Do you work here every day? I mean, should I tell somebody inside about it?

Jack: I'm unloading. That's all. Go around front if you need somebody.

Ann: Okay. I'll go around. Sorry about the mess.
