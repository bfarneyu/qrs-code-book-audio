Ruiz: Deputy Halvorsen will lead in the gray Crown Victoria. Stay four car lengths behind him. You're taking Ranch Road 12 west, across the river, then the county line highway.

Ann: Not the interstate. I've got enough gas. I checked before I came here.

Ruiz: Good. Don't stop for gas until you're across the county line. Pack at six. Bring what you need without making trips back to the duplex.

Ann: I'm taking the Civic so it isn't sitting outside Priya's window. What do I tell her when I leave?

Ruiz: The San Antonio internship explanation we discussed. Do not give her the cottage address. This is temporary witness security, not a visit she can arrange.

Ann: And the phone stays off? Not silent, actually powered off?

Ruiz: Off before you leave the city limits. No tower handoffs on your personal number. Check in with me from the cottage landline at eight.

Ann: Four car lengths behind the gray sedan. No gas stop here. Phone off, then call you at eight from the landline. Okay. I've got it.
