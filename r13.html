From The QRS Code Book by Bryan Farney

Transcript

Edith: I was promised a puppy demonstration, not a demonstration of my ability to stand in the sun. Marcus, shall we show them someone who actually follows instructions?

Marcus: We will, Mrs. Caldwell. First, everybody keep a little space along the fence. He needs a clear path back to me, not a tunnel of reaching hands.

Susan: Grandmother, let me take your bag. The photographer wants you beside the sign afterward, and the committee is waiting for a picture before they leave.

Edith: The committee can wait until the puppy has finished his education. Mine has taken eighty-one years, and nobody has offered me a certificate.

Marcus: I'm opening this run, then bringing him back across the yard. Keep your hands where they are, please. Give me one moment with this gate.

Susan: Not on her skirt. Those little paws have been in the dirt. Here, Grandmother, turn this way before he gets all the way up.

Marcus: He's fine. Let him settle before you reach down. That's it. Quiet hands tell him more than five people giving him five different orders.

Edith: A useful principle. Perhaps you could give the committee a lesson after their photograph.
