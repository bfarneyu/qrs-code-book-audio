Paul: Start with what you personally observed. Not what Frank remembers, not what you found online. Who supplied the envelope at the mill?

Ann: Susan took it out of her bag. James handed it over. The man from the Malibu looked through the pages.

Paul: And the identification of James and Susan came afterward, from photographs you compared with the people you saw?

Ann: Yes. Susan in the Courier announcement. James on his firm's website. Frank gave me his name first.

Paul: Good. The recordings support a forgery arrangement. They don't give you permission to turn every suspicion into a fact. Did you see anyone put a document in Edith Caldwell's house?

Ann: No. We saw the gate from across the road. We didn't go in.

Paul: Then placement hasn't been established. Neither has a completed theft. The truck photograph is partial. Frank's recognition is a witness identification, not a plate lookup.

Ann: But you believe there's something here? I'm not just hearing what I want to hear?

Paul: There's something here. Now we take the originals to someone who can corroborate it.
