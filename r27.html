Transcript

Zach: Ann, wait until morning. You don't need the charger tonight.

Ann: Five minutes. Priya's Honda is right in the driveway, I won't even go in if the lights are fine, I'll text you from the door.

Zach: Then I'll drive. We can both go.

Ann: And leave the computer here with nobody watching it? Stay with the backups. I need to tell Priya to lock up.

Zach: Fifteen minutes. Contact me, even if you don't go inside.

Ann: I will. I'm getting a charger, not investigating anything.
