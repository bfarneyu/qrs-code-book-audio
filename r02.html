Zach: Okay, I'm talking normally. Keep the center of the dish pointed at my chest, not my face.

Ann: I can hear you. That's ridiculous. You look farther away than you sound.

Zach: Now turn it a little toward the pasture. Just your wrists. Hear how the consonants disappear?

Ann: Yes. You're still there, but I can't understand you. Wait, now you're back.

Zach: That's the aiming cone. Hold it steady. I'm going to close the car door. Don't change the gain.

Ann: Okay, that hurt. Next time give me more warning.

Zach: That's why we're testing before Friday. The recorder needs room for sudden peaks.

Ann: Fine. No moving wrists. No surprise doors.
