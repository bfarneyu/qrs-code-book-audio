Transcript

Ann: Did somebody get Ned's phone off the crate? He turned it on before James came upstairs. Please don't lose that.

Ruiz: The evidence sergeant has it. You don't have to guard it now. Let the medics finish your wrists.

Ann: And Jack? He knows where we live.

Ruiz: In custody. They picked him up beside his truck on Elm Street.

Ann: Susan too? Does Edith know?

Ruiz: Susan was packing her suitcases when the deputies knocked, and her grandmother made them all a pot of coffee.
