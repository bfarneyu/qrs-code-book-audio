Transcript

Ned: The first price covers the household paper. Now, the instruction...

James: Not out here. You have what you need for that. Tell me the total, and tell me when you can deliver.

Ned: Half now, half when you accept both. Changes cost extra. If the samples aren't usable, the clock stops until you bring me something better.

Susan: They're usable. I checked every page. We can't spend another week going back and forth because you decide you want a different pen.

Ned: Then hear the terms. Two papers, using the samples you supplied. Your people handle placement. I don't enter the house. Is that what you're paying for? Say yes plainly.

James: Yes. That is what I am paying for. The household details come from the man in the pickup, not from anyone else.

Ned: The house man is coming to my hotel tonight. I'll settle the details with him there. You two go first. He's already pulling away.

Susan: Get in the car. Please, just get in.
