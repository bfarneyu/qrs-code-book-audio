Listen: First report to Detective Ruiz

From The QRS Code Book by Bryan Farney

Transcript

Ruiz: Miss Delgado, I'm taking your report seriously. That does not mean I can make an arrest on your interpretation of a conversation.

Ann: You heard him discussing the handwriting samples. And the man in the truck brought the household schedule to the hotel.

Ruiz: I heard the recordings. I will check the people, the hotel information, and what can be independently verified. Your job is not to obtain the next piece for me.

Ann: What if they put the paper in the house before you finish checking? Susan said something about Friday at the gala.

Ruiz: Is that statement on a saved recording?

Ann: No. I heard it, but I didn't get the app open in time. I can tell you exactly where I was standing.

Ruiz: Then we document it as your recollection. Don't call it recorded evidence. Keep the devices and original files intact. Don't edit them, rename over them, or discard anything.

Ann: Should Zach and I watch the road, just in case something happens? We wouldn't go onto the property.

Ruiz: No. No surveillance, no following cars, no hotel doors. Do not approach James Whitlock, Susan Caldwell, or either man you recorded. Go back to your classes. Let me do the investigation.

Ann: All right. I hear you.
