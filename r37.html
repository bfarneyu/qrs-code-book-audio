Transcript

James: Why is she standing there? I have called you six times. You were supposed to answer, not make me drive out here and find this.

Ned: Stay on that step. You can talk from there. She's standing because I cut the ties. Her hands were swelling up. That wasn't part of your bargain either.

James: Then finish the bargain: do it tonight, make it look like she ran. Take what she has and bring me the files. We have already discussed the price.

Ned: You discussed a price. I told you I don't do murder. Pointing that thing at me doesn't turn what you want into something I agreed to.

James: Where does Zach keep the backups? Don't look at him. Look at me. Tell me where they are, and give me the passwords.

Ann: That won't fix it. Paul has Marcus's recording. Detective Ruiz has the bank custodian. Those aren't my files. You can't get them back by taking something from me.

James: You don't know what they have. You heard pieces and decided you understood. Ned, step aside. I am giving you one last opportunity to walk down those stairs.

Ned: Not with you behind me holding a gun. Lower it. There's still a way you leave this room without adding another charge.

James: I can shoot you both and leave it in your hand. Think about whose story they will believe.

Ann: Don't turn. Keep your hands where they can see them. Please, James. Put it down.
