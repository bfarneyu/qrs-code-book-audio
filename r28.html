Transcript

Paul: It's twelve-fifteen Saturday morning. Zach, separate what you saw from what Priya told you. Start with Ann leaving your apartment.

Zach: We argued about it at ten. She wanted her laptop charger and wanted to check Priya. She said she'd contact me from the door. Her phone was nearly dead.

Paul: The argument isn't an arrival time. What can you fix from your call log?

Zach: At eleven-fifteen I called Ann. Voicemail. At eleven-twenty-five I called Priya. She went out to the carport while we were talking.

Paul: What did she report seeing? Don't supply anything she didn't say.

Zach: Ann's Civic. Driver's door open. Phone and keys in the gravel. The gray sedan wasn't beside the Honda anymore. Priya went back inside, locked the door, and called nine-one-one.

Paul: Priya's earlier sighting puts that sedan there at ten-thirty. Ruiz already has Garza's registration from the motel. That's a lead, not a guess about where he went.

Zach: She left the files with me. She didn't take the laptop. Whatever he wanted, she didn't have it.
