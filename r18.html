Transcript

James: Roy, I understand the amount. Nine hundred thousand dollars. I have not forgotten what I owe you and Curtis. I am asking for three more weeks.

James: No. Coming to my office will not produce the money. Let me be clear. I have a transfer being arranged. You will get a payment, but I need the time.

James: I understand. I heard you the first time. I will call you.

Ned: You look like hell. First paper's finished. Friday, May tenth, like you wanted. That doesn't mean I'm doing placement.

James: Nobody is asking you to. There is a two-point-four-million-dollar hole in her accounts. Crestline was paper covering losses for other clients. Now she wants real money for the Center.

Ned: Your accounts aren't my job. Tell me when your people need the paper in the house.

James: The house is Thursday.
