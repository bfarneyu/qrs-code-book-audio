Transcript

Edith: Put the forms beside the blue cup, dear. Not on the floor. Pearl believes anything on the floor has been legally transferred to her.

Ann: Marcus marked the places that need initials. I put a spare sheet behind each one because the pen was coming through the paper.

Emily: That was thoughtful. Mrs. Caldwell likes to keep a clean copy. Would you care for milk, or shall I leave your tea plain?

Ann: Plain, thank you. Has Susan always liked dogs too? What did you call her when she was little? Did she have a family nickname?

Emily: Mrs. Caldwell stopped calling Susan Susie-Q years ago. Twenty years, I should think. Susan asked her to stop, and that was the end of it.

Edith: She considered it undignified. A child in patent shoes explaining dignity to me. I respected her wishes, although I did keep the photograph of the shoes.

Ann: Sorry. That sounded like an interview. My mother still uses my whole name when she's worried, so I wondered how other families handled it.

Emily: Every family has its ways. Have another biscuit before Otto decides you aren't interested. He's been watching your plate since you sat down.

Edith: And then show me where to sign. Marcus may be patient with puppies, but he expects the paperwork back.
