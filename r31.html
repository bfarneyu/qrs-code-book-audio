Transcript

Ann: You met them here before. Down by the loading dock. Is that why you brought me to the mill?

Ned: I brought you somewhere the car isn't sitting in an open field at daylight. Don't start asking me about every turn we took.

Ann: You said morning. You said I could go home when he got his files. It isn't the same job anymore, is it?

Ned: The job was two papers. A will and a transfer instruction. Then an overnight hold until he dealt with your computer. That's what he paid for.

Ann: And now he wants something else. I heard you tell him you don't do that part.

Ned: He changes the rules when his own mess catches up with him. Thinks the money he already spent buys every risk he can invent. It doesn't buy murder.

Ann: Then let me go. You can stop this part yourself.

Ned: Sit still. I haven't figured out how I walk out of it. You talking isn't making that easier.
