Listen: Marcus and Susan at the Center

From The QRS Code Book by Bryan Farney

Transcript

Marcus: Before we discuss the tent invoices, Susan, I need you to look at this reply from the custodian bank. Mrs. Caldwell authorized the inquiry in writing.

Susan: I know she gave you a letter. What exactly did you ask them? Because James handles several different accounts, and you can confuse people by using the wrong name.

Marcus: I used the name printed on her statements. Crestline Income Fund. They checked it twice. No fund by that name exists there. The statements don't match the accounts they hold for her.

Susan: You're a hired dog trainer, Marcus. You don't understand this family's affairs. You can't wave a fax around and decide you understand my grandmother's money.

Marcus: Then explain the difference. The Center is expecting a million and a half before summer. Who is going to supply it? Can James produce a statement the bank recognizes?

Susan: Ask James. I came here about invoices, not to stand in your office being cross-examined. Have you upset her with this already?

Marcus: I work for Mrs. Caldwell. She asked me a question, and I intend to answer it.

Susan: Then tell her you don't know enough to answer it. I'm going through the yard.

Marcus: Mind that gate. The collar sticks. Wait there, I'll call him back.
