Transcript

Ned: Reiner? Ned Garza. I've got Ann Delgado. She's alive. She gave me this number. Don't send a bunch of kids with rifles running up here.

Paul: Stay on the line. Detective Ruiz is joining us. I'm recording this call. Where is Ann right now?

Ruiz: Mr. Garza, let me hear her voice. Hold the phone where she can speak without you answering for her.

Ned: She's right here. Tell her you're breathing, kid. Then give it back.

Ann: Detective Ruiz? I'm okay. My wrists hurt. I can walk. Please come get me. We're upstairs. He's holding the phone for me.

Ruiz: I hear you, Ann. Mr. Garza, name the building. Keep your hands away from any weapon.

Ned: Greenwood Mill. River Road. Malibu's inside the loading bay. I've recorded Whitlock since the first meeting. I want that counted.

Ruiz: Only the district attorney can negotiate an agreement. You will be taken into custody for kidnapping and forgery. Cooperation does not erase that. Keep Ann unharmed.

Paul: You say you recorded both of them from the beginning, Garza. How do we know you have Susan Caldwell on the forgery and not just Whitlock?

Ned: Because I kept the wording meeting. Listen to this.
