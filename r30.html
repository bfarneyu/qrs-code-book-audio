Listen: After the alarm

From The QRS Code Book by Bryan Farney

Transcript

Ruiz: Marcus, confirm the count for me. Every intake dog and everyone working tonight is accounted for?

Marcus: Twenty intake puppies. My shepherd. The handlers. All safe inside the clinic when it started. Nobody was in the trailer.

Ruiz: Did you go near the trailer door after the fire started?

Marcus: No. I kept everyone behind the fire doors. I could see the steps burning from the clinic window. My boots were still outside on the mat.

Ruiz: There was a steel bar wedged under the outside latch handle. Don't touch anything when the fire crew clears it. The investigators need it as it stands.

Marcus: Then whoever did this meant to keep me inside. This wasn't somebody trying to scare me away from the accounts.

Ruiz: We'll document it. Stay with the dogs. Nobody goes out into the pasture.

Marcus: Tell Paul his warning got through. Tell him every dog is alive. And if there's anything I can do to help find Ann, ask me.
