Transcript

Ned: You could have come upstairs. Now I've got to read this under a parking light. Which side is the study?

Jack: Left, coming down the hall. Don't go by the windows. Old lady sits there sometimes after supper, just... watching.

Ned: I don't go inside. You do. Is this her schedule or somebody's guess?

Jack: It's right. Unless she changes it. Can't put that on paper.

Ned: Then answer when I call. No more making me chase you downstairs.

Jack: Don't call the house. Use this number.
